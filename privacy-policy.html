1. Introduction

Socios Community ("we", "us", "our") is committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, share, and protect your personal information when you use our website, purchase tickets, or attend our events (collectively, the "Service").

This Privacy Policy complies with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and other applicable data protection laws in Hungary and the European Union.

Contact Information: talktosocios@gmail.com

By using our Service, you consent to the data practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Personal Information You Provide

We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us when you:

  • Register for an account
  • Purchase event tickets
  • Sign up for our newsletter or mailing list
  • Contact us for customer support
  • Participate in surveys or promotions
  • Attend our events

This information may include:

  • Identity Information: Full name, date of birth
  • Contact Information: Email address, phone number, mailing address
  • Financial Information: Billing address (payment card details are processed by our third-party payment processor and are not stored by us)
  • Event Information: Event attendance history, ticket purchases, preferences
  • Communication Data: Your communications with us, feedback, and survey responses

2.2 Information We Collect Automatically

When you use our Service, we automatically collect certain information about your device and usage patterns:

  • Technical Data: IP address, browser type and version, device type, operating system, time zone setting
  • Usage Data: Information about how you use our website, including pages visited, time spent on pages, links clicked, and other navigation actions
  • Location Data: General geographic location based on your IP address

2.3 Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar tracking technologies to collect information about your browsing behaviour. Please see Section 10 for more details.

3. How We Collect Your Information

We collect personal data through the following methods:

  • Direct Interactions: When you fill out forms on our website, purchase tickets, subscribe to newsletters, or contact us
  • Automated Technologies: Through cookies, server logs, and similar technologies as you navigate our website
  • Third Parties: From payment processors, analytics providers, and marketing platforms
  • Event Attendance: Through registration, check-in processes, and photography/videography at events

4. How We Use Your Information

4.1 Legal Bases for Processing (GDPR)

We process your personal data on the following legal bases:

  • Consent: You have given clear consent for us to process your personal data for specific purposes
  • Contract Performance: Processing is necessary to fulfill our contractual obligations to you
  • Legal Obligation: Processing is necessary to comply with legal requirements
  • Legitimate Interests: Processing is necessary for our legitimate business interests

4.2 Purposes of Processing

We use your personal information for the following purposes:

  • Processing and fulfilling event bookings and ticket purchases
  • Managing event registrations and attendance
  • Providing customer support and responding to inquiries
  • Sending transactional communications (booking confirmations, event updates, cancellations)
  • Administering and managing our business operations
  • Detecting, preventing, and addressing fraud and security issues
  • Sending promotional emails about upcoming events (with your consent)
  • Complying with legal obligations and responding to legal requests

5. Data Sharing & Disclosure

5.1 Third-Party Service Providers

We share your personal data with trusted third party service providers who assist us in operating our business:

  • Payment Processors: To process credit card and debit card payments securely (e.g., Stripe, PayPal)
  • Email Marketing Services: To send newsletters and promotional communications (e.g., Mailchimp, SendGrid)
  • Analytics Providers: To analyze website traffic and user behavior (e.g., Google Analytics)
  • Event Management Platforms: To manage ticket sales, registrations, and event logistics
  • Cloud Storage Providers: To store data securely

5.2 Event Venues and Partners

We may share limited personal information with event venues and co-hosting partners to facilitate event logistics, security, and venue requirements.

5.3 Legal Requirements

We may disclose your personal information if required to do so by law or in response to valid requests by public authorities.

5.4 Business Transfers

In the event of a merger, acquisition, reorganization, or sale of assets, your personal data may be transferred to the acquiring entity. You will be notified of any such change.

5.5 Data Not Sold

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for their marketing purposes.

6. Data Storage & Security

6.1 Data Storage

Your personal data is stored on secure servers provided by reputable cloud service providers. We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your data.

6.2 Security Measures

  • Encryption of data in transit using SSL/TLS protocols
  • Secure authentication and access controls
  • Regular security assessments and updates
  • Limited access to personal data on a need-to-know basis

6.3 Data Retention

  • Active Users: Personal data is retained as long as your account is active
  • Inactive Users: Data will be deleted after 2 years of inactivity unless required for legal or regulatory reasons
  • Transaction Records: Financial and transaction data may be retained for up to 7 years
  • Marketing Data: Retained only to the extent necessary to honor your opt-out preference

You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us at talktosocios@gmail.com.

6.4 Data Breach Notification

In the event of a data breach that poses a risk to your rights and freedoms, we will notify you and the relevant supervisory authority within 72 hours as required by GDPR.

7. Your Data Protection Rights (GDPR)

7.1 Right of Access

You have the right to request access to the personal data we hold about you.

7.2 Right to Rectification

You have the right to request correction of any inaccurate or incomplete personal data we hold about you.

7.3 Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten")

You have the right to request deletion of your personal data in certain circumstances.

7.4 Right to Restriction of Processing

You have the right to request that we restrict processing of your personal data in certain circumstances.

7.5 Right to Data Portability

You have the right to receive your personal data in a structured, commonly used, and machine-readable format.

7.6 Right to Object

You have the right to object to processing of your personal data where we rely on legitimate interests as the legal basis.

7.7 Right to Withdraw Consent

Where processing is based on consent, you have the right to withdraw your consent at any time.

7.8 Right to Lodge a Complaint

You have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. In Hungary, the relevant authority is the National Authority for Data Protection and Freedom of Information (NAIH).

7.9 Exercising Your Rights

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at talktosocios@gmail.com. We will respond within one month.

8. International Data Transfers

8.1 Cross-Border Transfers

Your personal data may be transferred to and processed in countries outside the European Economic Area (EEA).

8.2 Safeguards

  • Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs): Approved by the European Commission
  • Adequacy Decisions: Transfers to countries deemed by the EU to provide adequate data protection
  • Privacy Shield (where applicable): For transfers to certified U.S. organizations
  • Service Provider Commitments: Binding agreements with third party processors requiring GDPR-compliant data protection

8.3 Third Party Services

Some of our service providers may be located outside the EEA. We ensure these providers comply with GDPR requirements through appropriate data processing agreements.

9. Marketing Communications

9.1 Consent

We will only send you marketing communications if you have provided explicit consent to receive them.

9.2 Opt-Out

You can opt out of receiving marketing communications at any time by:

  • Clicking the unsubscribe link in any marketing email
  • Manually opting out of text messages
  • Contacting us at talktosocios@gmail.com
  • Updating your communication preferences in your account settings

9.3 Transactional Communications

Even if you opt out of marketing communications, we will still send you essential transactional emails related to your bookings.

10. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

10.1 What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit our website. They help us recognize your device, remember your preferences, and improve your user experience.

10.2 Types of Cookies We Use

  • Essential Cookies: Required for the website to function properly. Cannot be disabled
  • Analytics Cookies: Help us understand how visitors interact with our website
  • Functionality Cookies: Remember your preferences and settings
  • Marketing Cookies: Track your browsing activity across websites for targeted advertising

10.3 Third Party Cookies

Some cookies on our website are set by third party services. We do not control these cookies, and you should refer to the respective third party privacy policies for more information.

10.4 Cookie Consent

When you first visit our website, you will be asked to consent to the use of non-essential cookies. You can change your cookie preferences at any time through the cookie settings banner.

Our Service is not intended for individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal data from minors. Our events are restricted to attendees aged 18 and above.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at talktosocios@gmail.com.

12. Third Party Websites

Our website may contain links to third party websites, social media platforms, or services that are not operated by Socios Community. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of these third party sites.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

13.1 Right to Modify

We reserve the right to update or modify this Privacy Policy at any time to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements.

13.2 Notification of Changes

  • Sending an email to the address associated with your account
  • Posting a prominent notice on our website
  • Updating the Last Updated date at the top of this policy

13.3 Continued Use

Your continued use of the Service after changes to this Privacy Policy constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.

14. Data Protection Officer

While Socios Community is not currently required to appoint a Data Protection Officer under GDPR, we take data protection seriously.

For any questions, concerns, or requests related to your personal data, please contact us at: talktosocios@gmail.com
