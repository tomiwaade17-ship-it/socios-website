About Socios

Socios is a social gathering aimed at fostering mingling and interactions based on shared interests.

The event provides a space for young professionals and creatives from diverse backgrounds to come together and enjoy music, art, conversation, and other forms of entertainment.

Why Socios?

Professionals and creatives often lack in person opportunities for meaningful connections in a relaxed, accessible setting. Despite a vibrant cultural scene in Budapest, barriers to networking and collaboration persist.

What To Expect At Socios

Connect Through Shared Interests

Our events are designed to spark dialogue. Discover fellow enthusiasts through shared appreciation for culture, art, and music.

Enjoy The Atmosphere

Savour exquisite drinks, gourmet bites, and the sophisticated backdrop of Brody House. Let the unique setting enhance your evening.

Leave Inspired

Depart with new insights, expanded networks, and a refreshed perspective, already looking forward to the next gathering.

Our Vision & Mission

At Socios, we seek to create a vibrant and engaging social experience where attendees can network, share ideas, and form new connections. By focusing on shared interests—whether in art, music, or culture—the event promotes authentic interactions for young professionals and creatives.
