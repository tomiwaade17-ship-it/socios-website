Frequently Asked Questions

How frequently do we have Socios events?

We organise events periodically and usually announce upcoming events 1-2 months prior.

To keep updated on upcoming events, see the Events page or follow our social media page on your preferred platforms for ongoing updates.

Is the dress-code mandatory?

For events with a suggested dress-code, guests are expected to honour them. If the dresscode is mandatory, it will be stated as 'Dress Code Mandatory' on the ads/ flyers for the event. Non-compliance could lead to denied entry to the event.

I'd like to support, collaborate/ partner with Socios

We are always open to collaborations and partnerships. Please reach out to us directly at talktosocios@gmail.com with your proposal and we'll get back to you.

I had a bad experience during an event, where can I raise my complaint?

We're sorry to hear that. Please email us at talktosocios@gmail.com with details of your experience and we will address it promptly.
